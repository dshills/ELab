# Captured preview lifecycle and image discovery

Four on-demand contracts now cover preview Start, Cancel, Close and Image, alongside the existing page-switch contract. Discovery separates source freshness from visibility/status/retained-byte readiness; complete page overrides from defaults; queued admission from rendered/decoded pixels; and cooperative cancellation from actual worker return. Refresh replaces the old ID even on later failure. Close retains metadata but releases bytes, while repeated close is allowed. Image uses inclusive EOF and nullable/default offset/limit semantics, exact IDs and whole-image checksums.

The page-bounds helper is shared with exports under a general name. All six previously qualified export schemas compare exactly equal; ordinary catalog and output/shared schemas stay unchanged. There are no renderer, numerical, model, work-limit, command-ID or file-format changes.
