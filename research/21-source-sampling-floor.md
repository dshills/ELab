The public example uses R = 1 kΩ, C = 1 nF, initial voltage zero and a 0→5 V ramp over T = 1 µs. With τ = RC = 1 µs, the exact first-order response during the ramp is `5/T × [t + τ × expm1(−t/τ)]`. After T, it is `5 − [5 − v(T)] × exp[−(t−T)/τ]`. This follows directly from `C dv/dt = (u−v)/R`; it is independent of ELab's integrator.

The output grid retains six rows over 4 µs: 0, both stored sides of the continuous 1 µs ramp corner, 2, 3 and 4 µs. Source voltage, capacitor voltage and resistor current are checked against the analytic solution. At the ramp end, the reference is 5/e ≈1.839397 V. Halfway through the ramp, the reference is ≈0.532653 V; a straight segment joining only the stored endpoints would be ≈0.919689 V. Internally accurate stored values do not establish that the displayed curve resolves its intervening response. The heuristic remains explicitly unresolved; lengthening the rise to 4 µs makes its target equal the supported floor.
