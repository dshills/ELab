# Participant task cards

Moderator: show one card at a time. Keep the scoring guide and answer key separate. These are study prompts, not participant observations.

## T1 · A small light circuit

Start with this empty sheet. Make a circuit with a 5 V supply, a red LED, a 1 kΩ resistor and ground. Find its current reading. Then change the resistor to 2 kΩ and observe what happens to the current. Work as you normally would and tell us what you are looking for.

## T2 · Explain the change

What happened when you changed the resistance? Why do you think that happened?

## T6a · Keep your experiment

Keep this circuit somewhere you can find it later. We will use other test circuits and come back to yours.

## T3 · A changing voltage

This resistor-capacitor circuit is ready to run. Make it show the capacitor's voltage over time. Choose a time visible on that trace and enter the time to inspect the voltage there.

## T4a · A disconnected circuit

A connection has been removed from your light circuit. Get it working again while keeping the experiment you made.

## T4b · An accidental deletion

A part has now been removed accidentally. Return to the complete circuit you had before that deletion.

## T5 · Can you use this reading?

For each of these three conditions: is the simulation progressing, holding a usable result, or unable to give a current result for the circuit on the sheet? What tells you that? Would you use the displayed readings for this circuit now?

## T6b · Return and explain a limit

Return to the experiment you kept earlier. Check that its values and connections are still what you intended. Find one limitation or assumption that matters when using its simulation result for real parts.

## Debrief

What was most confusing? What helped you understand the circuit? How confident are you about repeating this workflow, and how satisfied were you with the experience? What would you change first?
