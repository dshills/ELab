Make yourself at home
Try the same tasks in the native app and ELab Web.
  1. Build a light.
    Start a new drawing. Add a DC source, resistor, red LED and ground. Connect the terminals, then change the resistor to 2 kΩ.
  2. Explore an RC circuit.
    Open RC charge/discharge. Change the capacitor, record a run, and place two cursors on the plot.
  3. Reverse a current.
    Open Four-MOSFET H-bridge. Pause and step across 20 ms. Compare load voltage and current.
  4. Take your work with you.
    Download the project, open it again, and export its schematic.
